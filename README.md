# Privacy Policy — Proper Productions

**Last updated: March 2026**

## Overview
Proper Productions operates a personal automation tool for managing our own Pinterest business account. This policy describes how data is handled.

## Data Collection
We do not collect personal data from any users. This tool is used exclusively by Proper Productions to manage our own Pinterest and Etsy accounts.

## Third-Party Services
This tool integrates with:
- **Pinterest API** — to schedule and publish pins to our own account
- **Etsy API** — to read our own product listings

No data from these services is stored, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Cookies
This tool does not use cookies or tracking technologies.
